# Product

This is a professional-assistance research workbench, not an autonomous diagnosis or crisis-response product. Its primary journey is 20 fixed seed probes -> auditable automatic scoring -> evidence sufficiency check -> one minimal adaptive probe when needed -> confirmation or expert review.

The UI exposes five views: Assessment, Evidence Map, Research Dashboard, Expert Review, and Case Replay. A score of 2 is an item-level rubric result and is never presented as equivalent to clinical high risk.

After the twentieth Seed Probe, the participant sees a session-level handoff
rather than another item score: a warm summary, construct-level evidence
density, the number of bounded probes already used, and any nodes that remain
uncertain. They can open the full Evidence Map or replay their answers. If a
safety gate is active, automatic navigation and companion probing stop and the
session remains in the professional workflow.
