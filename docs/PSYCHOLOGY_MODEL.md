# Psychology Model

The DOCX defines item-level constructs and 0/1/2 descriptions. Its dimension notes are exploratory and must not be treated as validated factor structure. The system therefore reports item scores and evidence status, not a total clinical risk score.

Key research distinction: absence of observed evidence is not evidence of absence. A response may have `preliminary_score = 0` while remaining `PROVISIONAL` with `evidence_sufficiency = INSUFFICIENT`. Rubric conflicts, construct changes, and safety/clinical interpretation require review by the psychology team.
